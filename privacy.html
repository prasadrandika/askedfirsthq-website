Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-27.

What we collect

When you ask for a report we receive your name, email, business name, type of business, city and website. When you become a client we also receive the business details you send us for the fixes.

Public business information

To prepare reports we use information that businesses publish themselves, such as their website, business email and public listings, and the answers AI assistants give to customer questions.

How we use it

Form requests are stored in our Google account (Google Sheets) and answered from our Gmail. We use your details only to prepare and send your report, deliver the services you ask for, and reply to you. We don't sell or rent your information and we don't use it for advertising.

Emails

If we contact a business that hasn't contacted us, it is a single message with a free report and an easy way to opt out. If you reply "unsubscribe" or "stop", we never email you again.

Payments

Card payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

How long we keep it

We keep report data while you are a client and for up to 24 months after, so we can show progress. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.

Your choices

Email hello@askedfirsthq.com to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop all emails.

This website

This site doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
