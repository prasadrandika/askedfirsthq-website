How to get more reviews without feeling pushy

Simple, honest ways for local businesses to get more customer reviews, which AI assistants use when recommending businesses.

Reviews help customers trust you, and they help AI assistants decide who to recommend. The good news is that most happy customers are glad to leave one. They just need a simple nudge at the right moment.

Ask at the right time

The best moment is right after the job is done and the customer is pleased. A short text that evening works better than an email a week later.

Make it one tap

Send a direct link to your review page. Every extra step loses people.

A message you can copy

Hi Sarah, thanks for having us out today. If you were happy with the work, a quick review would really help a small local business like ours: [link]. Thank you!

What not to do

  • Never write reviews yourself or pay for them. It breaks platform rules and can get your listing removed.
  • Don't offer discounts in exchange for positive reviews.
  • Reply to every review, good or bad, briefly and politely.
