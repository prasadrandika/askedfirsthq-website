Terms and refunds

Last updated 2026-11-26. Plain language, no surprises.

The service

AskedFirst checks how AI assistants answer customer questions about local businesses, prepares fixes, and re-checks every month. We work by email.

No guarantee of results

AI assistants are run by other companies and their answers change. We can't control or guarantee what they say. Our reports show what the assistants answered on the date checked.

Free report

Free, with no obligation. One per business.

Fix Pack ($49, one time)

Delivered by email within 3 business days after payment and after you send the details we ask for. You may use everything we deliver for your own business, even if you stop working with us.

Done For You ($249, one time)

Everything in the Fix Pack, plus we make the changes with you within 7 business days after you send the details and access we ask for (for example, adding us as a manager on your Google Business Profile). We never ask for your passwords. Includes one re-check 30 days later.

Growth ($79) and Pro ($199) per month, with progress

Each month we re-check the same questions. We send a payment link only if your score is higher than the previous month. There is no automatic subscription and no minimum term. Pro covers 25 questions and up to 2 locations or service lines, plus monthly outreach to local guides.

Multi-City ($1,999 per month, with progress)

Covers up to 5 cities with 10 questions each, Done For You setup in every city, and monthly outreach. We send a payment link only in months where the total score across all cities is higher than the month before.

One business per trade per city

While you are an active client, we won't take on another business in the same trade and city. The slot is released after your last paid month if you stop.

Refunds

Fix Pack: full refund within 14 days if you haven't used it. Done For You: full refund within 14 days if we haven't started. Growth and Pro: if you think a score increase was a mistake, reply within 7 days for a full refund. Email hello@askedfirsthq.com.

Your responsibilities

You decide what goes on your website and listings. Please check that the facts we prepare (prices, hours, licences) are correct before publishing them.
