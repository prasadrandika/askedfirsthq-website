About AskedFirst

We started AskedFirst after noticing that good local businesses were simply missing from AI answers, while owners had no easy way to even check.

What we believe

Being recommended should depend on doing good work and being easy to understand online, not on a big marketing budget.

Most of what AI assistants need is simple: clear information on your own website, the same details everywhere, and real customers saying good things. We do the tedious parts and show you the results honestly.

How we work

We are a small, remote team. We use AI tools to run the checks and draft the fixes quickly, and every report goes through our quality checklist before it reaches you.

We work by email only. It keeps things clear, written down and easy to fit around a busy day, and it means we can serve local businesses across time zones: the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand.

Our rules

  • No fake reviews, testimonials or case studies
  • No promises of "#1" or guaranteed rankings
  • Every score shows the questions, the AI assistants and the date
  • One business per trade in each city
  • We never sell your data
  • Not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Perplexity or any AI company
