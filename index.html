    Questions owners ask

    Short, straight answers. More on the FAQ page.

    Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

    No, and you should be careful with anyone who says they can. AI answers change from week to week. What we can do is measure the same 10 questions every month, fix what is missing, and show you the before and after. That is also why the monthly fee is only charged in months your score goes up.

    Which AI assistants do you check?

    We check the assistants local customers use, such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Perplexity. Every report lists exactly which assistants were checked and on which date, so you can repeat any question yourself.

    How do you choose the 10 questions?

    They are written for your trade and city and match how real customers ask: emergencies, prices, reviews, opening hours and the jobs you do most. You can see a full set on the sample report page.

    Do I have to change my website?

    Usually a little. Most fixes are text and a small block of code that we prepare for you. You paste them in, or forward them to whoever looks after your site.

    Will you call me or ask for passwords?

    No. Everything happens by email so you can read it when you have time. We never ask for your website or Google passwords; you or your web person make the changes.
