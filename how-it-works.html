How it works

A simple, repeatable method. The same questions every month, so you can see real change instead of guesses.

1

Write the questions

We write 10 questions for your trade and city, based on how customers really ask: urgent help, price, reviews, hours, and your most common jobs.

2

Ask the AI assistants

We ask each question to AI assistants your customers use and record every answer, the date and which assistant answered.

3

Score it

Your score is the number of questions (out of 10) where you were named at least once. Simple enough to check yourself.

What we look at when you're missing

AI assistants build their answers from what the web says about you. When a business is left out, it is usually for one of these reasons:

  • Your website doesn't answer the questionIt doesn't clearly say what you do, where, when and for roughly how much.
  • Your details don't match across the webDifferent names, phone numbers or addresses on Google, Yelp, BBB and other listings.
  • Nobody points to youYou're not on local "best of" guides, association lists or community pages that AI trusts.
  • Reviews are thin or oldFew recent reviews, or reviews only in one place.

What the Fix Pack contains

  • Website text you can paste: a clear services page, a service-area page and 8 to 12 FAQ answers written for the questions you missed
  • LocalBusiness schema code (a small block that tells search engines and AI your hours, area and services)
  • A checklist of every listing to correct, with what to change
  • Review-request messages (text and email) for your real customers
  • A short list of local guides and directories worth being on

Every month after that

We ask the same 10 questions again and send a one-page report: last month's score, this month's score, what changed and what to do next.

If the score went up, we send a $79 payment link. If it stayed the same or went down, you pay nothing that month and we keep working.

AI answers vary. A single month can move up or down for reasons outside anyone's control, which is why we track the trend over several months.
