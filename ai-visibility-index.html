Checked 26–27 November 2026 with Google Gemini (free version, no live web search). Small first sample on one engine, so read it as an early signal, not a verdict on any business.

What we found

We checked 8 plumbers, 5 roofers, 2 electricians and 2 heating and cooling companies. All are independent and have their own websites. Fourteen of them were not named in any of their 10 answers. The other three scored 7, 4 and 1 out of 10.

All 5 Spokane roofers scored zero, and so did both Spokane electricians.

The answers concentrate. We asked the same set of plumbing questions for 5 Spokane plumbers, 50 answers in all, and one company appeared in 48 of them. AI tends to return a short list, and the same few names come back again and again.

The businesses that did get named tend to have clear service pages with the city on them, mentions on other local sites, and consistent listings and reviews. That's where we start every fix.

Method

  • 10 questions per business, written for its trade and city
  • Each question asked fresh, with no history, and saved with the date
  • Score = number of questions where the business is named at least once
  • Failed or incomplete checks are thrown away and repeated, never counted as zero
  • We don't publish the names of businesses that were left out, or of any business without permission
