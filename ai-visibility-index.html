Checked 26 November 2026 with Google Gemini. Small first sample; each edition adds cities and trades.

What we found

Both businesses we checked are independent, family-run plumbers with their own websites. Across 20 questions, including "Who is a trustworthy family-owned plumber in Boise?", the AI never mentioned either of them.

Instead it kept returning a small group of the same companies. One business was named in 4 of 10 answers for the same question set. That pattern, a few names repeated again and again, is what makes being on the list so valuable.

The answers leaned on review sites and local directories, which is why consistent listings are the first thing we fix.

Method

  • 10 questions per business, written for its trade and city
  • Each question asked fresh, with no history, and saved with the date
  • Score = number of questions where the business is named at least once
  • Failed or incomplete checks are thrown away and repeated, never counted as zero
  • We don't publish the names of businesses that were left out
