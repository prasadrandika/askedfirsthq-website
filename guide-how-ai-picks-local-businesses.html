How AI assistants choose which local businesses to recommend

A plain-English look at where ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity get their answers about local businesses, and what that means for you.

When someone asks an AI assistant for "a good plumber near me", the answer looks simple: a short list of names and a sentence about each. Behind it, the assistant is pulling together what the web says about local businesses and deciding who seems like a safe recommendation.

Where the information comes from

  • Business websites. A clear site that says what you do, where, and when is easy to quote.
  • Listings and directories. Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Angi, Healthgrades, Avvo and similar sites help confirm you are real and active.
  • Reviews. Recent, specific reviews in more than one place are a strong signal.
  • Mentions. Local news, "best of" guides, associations and community threads act like votes.

Why good businesses get left out

The most common reasons are ordinary: a website that never mentions the city, a phone number that differs between listings, or no presence on the local guides the assistant reads. None of these say anything about the quality of your work, and all of them can be fixed.

What to do first

  1. Make sure your homepage says your trade and city in the first sentence.
  2. Check your name, address and phone are identical everywhere.
  3. Add an FAQ that answers the questions customers really ask.
  4. Ask every happy customer for a review, in a simple text after the job.
