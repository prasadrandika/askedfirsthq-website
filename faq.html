Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email hello@askedfirsthq.com.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and you should be careful with anyone who says they can. AI answers change from week to week. What we can do is measure the same 10 questions every month, fix what is missing, and show you the before and after. That is also why the monthly fee is only charged in months your score goes up.

Which AI assistants do you check?

We check the assistants local customers use, such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Perplexity. Every report lists exactly which assistants were checked and on which date, so you can repeat any question yourself.

How do you choose the 10 questions?

They are written for your trade and city and match how real customers ask: emergencies, prices, reviews, opening hours and the jobs you do most. You can see a full set on the sample report page.

Do I have to change my website?

Usually a little. Most fixes are text and a small block of code that we prepare for you. You paste them in, or forward them to whoever looks after your site.

Will you call me or ask for passwords?

No. Everything happens by email so you can read it when you have time. We never ask for your website or Google passwords; you or your web person make the changes.

How long until my score changes?

AI assistants that search the web can pick up changes within a few weeks. Others update more slowly. We re-check monthly and report what moved and what didn't.

What does 'one business per trade in each city' mean?

If you are a plumber in Boise and you become a client, we won't take another plumber in Boise. Other trades in your city (an electrician, a roofer) are separate slots.

What is the difference between the Fix Pack and Done For You?

With the Fix Pack you get everything ready to use and you (or your web person) make the changes. With Done For You we make the changes with you: we update your Google Business Profile once you add us as a manager, submit listing corrections, and prepare your new pages so publishing is one click.

What if I want to stop?

Reply to any email and we stop. There is no contract and no cancellation fee.

Do you work outside the United States?

Yes. We work with local businesses worldwide, starting with English-speaking markets: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. Your questions are written for your own city and country, and prices are in US dollars.

Do you work with businesses that have several locations?

Yes. Pro covers 2 locations, Multi-City covers up to 5 cities, and franchises or groups with more locations get a custom plan. Your territory slot is protected in every city you cover.

Can marketing agencies resell AskedFirst?

Yes. Our partner program gives agencies white-label reports and wholesale pricing. See the partners page.

Who is behind AskedFirst?

A small, remote team that uses AI tools to run checks quickly and carefully. You can read more on the about page.
